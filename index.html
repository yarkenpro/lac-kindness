Kindness at Leppington Anglican College

At the heart of our College is a simple belief: how we treat one another matters. Together we grow in character, compassion, and community.

↓

Why Kindness Matters Here

A kind school is a strong school. When students feel valued and safe, they learn with confidence, support one another, and carry those values into the wider world.

Our Ethos

“Be kind to one another, tender-hearted, forgiving one another.” — Ephesians 4:32

One College, One Community

From Kindergarten to Year 12, every member of Leppington Anglican College is called to lead with kindness — in the classroom, on the field, and in our neighbourhood.
